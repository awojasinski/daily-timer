Hand-painted cozy Japanese animated film aesthetic inspired by Hayao Miyazaki: watercolor and gouache texture, delicate warm brown pencil lines, soft natural light, charming imperfect handmade shapes, beautifully observed material details. Not glossy 3D, not flat vector clipart. BOTTOM LEFT CELL: the exact same bread silhouette and proportions and eye placement, toasted golden brown, happy satisfied face, tiny rosy cheeks. BOTTOM RIGHT CELL: the exact same bread silhouette and proportions and eye placement, charred dark brown toast with a worried face and pale expressive eyes, no smoke and no flames (animated separately). All three bread sprites are isolated slices with no toaster, no arms or legs, no props. All four sprites are centered in their own cell, fully visible, with transparency all around. The bread is wider than tall, about 1.4:1, a thick charming sandwich slice. Each cell has one subject only. Rich painterly craft and visual storytelling in the small expressive faces. Preserve clean translucent alpha edges and clear silhouettes for display at about 130px wide.
